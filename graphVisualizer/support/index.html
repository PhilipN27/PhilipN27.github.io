Ask a question or request support

Get help with Graphform, report unexpected behavior, share accessibility feedback, or suggest an improvement. Support requests are handled in Graphform’s public GitHub issue tracker.

Open a support request View existing requests

What to include

Details that help us investigate

  1. A short description of the question or problem.
  2. The steps you took and what you expected to happen.
  3. Your device model, operating-system version, and Graphform version.
  4. Any error message you saw. Screenshots are helpful when they do not contain private data.
Questions and problems

Use the support request link for setup questions, app behavior, import, export, and accessibility.

Feature requests

Describe the workflow you are trying to complete and the result you need.

Protect your information

Support requests are public. Do not include passwords, private graph content, or other sensitive information.
