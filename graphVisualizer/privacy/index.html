Graphform for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Your graphs stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Last updated September 24, 2026 · Developer: Philip Andrew Nora

Graphform does not require an account and does not collect your projects or personal information. The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or Graphform cloud service.

Data stored on your iPhone

Graphform saves the graphs and project details you create in the app’s local storage. Recently deleted projects remain on your device for up to 24 hours so you can restore them, then are removed from the app’s local trash.

You can delete projects in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data from the device, subject to any backups managed by your device settings. Apple device or iCloud backups may include app data according to your settings; Graphform does not run an automatic cloud sync service.

Files you choose to import, export, or share

Graphform reads a project file only after you select it for import. Exports and shares happen only when you choose them. If you save a file to Files, iCloud Drive, or another service, or send it to another app, that destination handles the file under its own privacy practices. Graphform does not upload those files to a Graphform server.

Support and feedback

You can contact us through the Graphform support page. Support requests use a public GitHub issue tracker. Please do not post private graph content, passwords, or other sensitive information there. Information you choose to submit to GitHub is handled under GitHub’s privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

If Graphform’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new date. You can return to this URL to read the current policy.
