A direct path to rehearsal

From script to spoken line.

  1. 01

    Bring in the script

    Import a text-based PDF from Files or optionally from Google Drive. Review the extracted words and correct them before saving.

  2. 02

    Make it your part

    Choose your role and define Acts at exact points in the reviewed text. Your script stays organized the way you rehearse it.

  3. 03

    Practice your way

    Use cue-line flashcards, first letters or typed lines. Mark how you did yourself; practice is not an automatic grade.

Optional connection

Your Drive.
Your choice.

Google’s Drive read-only permission is broad: it can see and download files you can access, not just a single PDF. Script Study shows supported folders and PDFs and downloads only a PDF you select for local import.

The app does not upload, edit, delete or automatically sync Drive files. You can use Files and the local library without connecting Google.
