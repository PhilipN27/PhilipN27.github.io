Privacy Policy

Script Study is a local-first iPhone app for script rehearsal. This page explains what happens to scripts, practice data and optional Google Drive access.

Effective September 24, 2026

Local library

When you import a PDF, Script Study saves its own copy on your device, together with reviewed text, corrections, roles, Acts, cue choices, practice marks and historical test results. Your original source file is not changed.

Deleting the app removes its local library. Device backups may retain app data according to your iPhone settings. There is no Script Study server that stores your scripts, and the app does not offer in-app cloud backup.

Optional Google Drive access

If you choose to connect, Google asks you to grant drive.readonly. This permission can see and download files you can access across Drive; it is broader than a single-file grant. Script Study uses it to show supported folders and PDF entries and to download only a PDF you select for local import. The app does not upload, edit or delete Drive files, and it does not sync them in the background.

The Google Sign-In SDK handles authorization and tokens. Script Study may display the connected account name, but does not put account identity or tokens in saved script records. Disconnecting Google leaves PDFs already imported into the local library in place. Local sign-out clears this app’s Google credentials on the device; the separate revoke action asks Google to end the app’s access. You can also manage access in your Google Account.

Optional speech practice

Speech practice starts only after you tap Start speaking and grant the needed permissions. It requires on-device recognition support for your language and device, with no app cloud fallback. The app does not save microphone audio or raw speech transcripts. It may save bounded comparison counts and an explicit self-assessment for that practice attempt.

Services and SDK data

Connecting Drive sends authentication and Drive API requests to Google. Google’s privacy policy applies to its processing. The Google Sign-In SDK privacy declaration includes account identifiers and other device and usage categories, with some analytics purposes. Script Study does not add a separate analytics reporting feature or send script content to a Script Study backend.

Importing through the iOS Files provider may involve a third-party file provider you choose.

Use and sharing

Script Study uses Google Drive data only to provide the browsing and PDF import you request. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or train generalized AI models on it. Script PDFs, reviewed text, practice marks and tests are not shared with a Script Study server. Google and any file provider you select handle their own service traffic under their terms.

Contact and changes

For privacy or deletion questions, contact pan97g@gmail.com. This policy should be updated if the app’s data practices change.
